# Using the iPhone app

On iPhone, inku opens to a home screen with your saved works. From home you can draw from a short description, page through works, or share an image.

## Home

- Use **All** and **Starred** to switch between all works and works you have starred. Tap a work to open the viewer.
- The **⋯** menu includes **Select**, **Order**, **Group by**, **Display**, **Trash**, **Import work pack**, and **Refresh**.
- Pull the works down to reveal search. Tap the gear at the top right to open Settings.

## Draw

1. Tap **Draw** on home and enter a description. You can enter several lines and see an approximate sound count below the field.
2. Tap **Draw** in the sheet to create a new work with the **Describe** settings in **Drawing defaults**. **Draw in detail** takes what you typed to the Making screen.
3. The waiting page shows progress from description to instructions, score, and picture. Drawing continues if you go back. Tap the progress row on home or another screen to return to the waiting page. Tap **Stop** to stop drawing.
4. After the work is saved, the waiting page shows the picture building up and gives haptic feedback as it opens the viewer. Tap the picture during the build-up to skip it and open the viewer. The full picture appears at once if the build-up is not ready in time or **Show drawing motion** is off. If you are elsewhere in the app, use **View** on the **Drawing finished** banner. Outside the app, iOS can notify you if you have granted permission.

A successful save clears the home description if it still matches what you submitted. Any text you changed during the run stays in the field. The description also stays if drawing fails, stops, or needs review.

To draw from a photo, tap the camera on home and take a picture. The app draws from the photo using the **Picture** settings in **Drawing defaults**.

## View works

- When zoomed out, swipe left or right to page through works, or swipe down to close the viewer. Pinch to zoom. Tap once to toggle the controls. Double-tap the picture to switch to 3D when available.
- Use the controls below the work to star it, **Refine** it, or **Share** it.
- The **⋯** menu opens **Work details** and comments, Lineage, **Open in Paint**, **Export**, caption settings, and Trash actions. The comment’s **Save** button is below the comment field in **Work details**.

## Share

Tap **Share** in the viewer to open the iOS share sheet with the work’s share card as an image. Choose a destination offered by the sheet, such as LINE.

## Import a work pack

1. **From a Mac.** Select the works. Under **Export**, use **Export work pack** to save an `.inkupack` file, then AirDrop the file to your iPhone.
2. **From the Web.** Open inku in Chrome on your iPhone and use **Export work pack** under **Export** to download the file.
3. Open the received file with inku. You can also save it to Files and choose it via **⋯** → **Import work pack** on home. Review the preview and counts, then tap **Import**. If a drawing is in progress, wait for it to finish.

Matching works are skipped, and their stars and comments on the receiving device are kept. You can view, share, and star imported works. Work packs omit the source execution records that hold the saved drawing conditions. Refinement options that need those conditions, such as **Change the description**, are disabled for imported works.

## Settings

- Open **Display and operation** to choose **Text size** and the **Mascot shown while drawing**. **Show drawing motion** is on by default. Turn it off to hold the drawing mascot still and show the process rows and finished picture all at once.
- To draw with a ChatGPT plan, open **Sign in with ChatGPT**. Use **Model settings** to configure connections and models, and the **Describe** and **Picture** sections of **Drawing defaults** to choose the models and settings to draw with.
- **Database** lets you save a backup or set up automatic backups. The database backup lets you restore works, lineage, and working state. Connection settings and API keys are managed separately.

## Making screen

Open **Draw in detail** from the draw sheet or choose **Open in Paint** from the viewer’s **⋯** menu. The iPhone layout gives you the same core Making tools as the Mac, including editing descriptions or instructions, setting drawing conditions, and browsing Lineage. For the shared workflows, see [Creating Images](./image-creation.md).
