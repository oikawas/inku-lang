inku Android — Color

Every colour the app paints with, named by what it is for. The values are exactly the ones the screens used before the token layer existed: nothing was merged or rounded, so two roles that happen to share an ARGB keep two names.

What MaterialTheme exposes

The nine roles MaterialTheme.colorScheme exposes. 160 call sites read these indirectly, so a change here moves the whole app. The app is dark-only.
