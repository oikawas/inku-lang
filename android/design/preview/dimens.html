inku Android — Dimens

Every distance the app measures with. These are not on a 4dp grid — 22 of the 53 sit off it — because pulling them onto one would move the drawing. That work belongs to the stage that rebuilds the screens. Bars are drawn to scale, clamped at 320dp.
